# Cross-Platform Demoscene Comparison

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

What can the ZX Spectrum do that the C64, Amiga, or Atari ST can't — and vice versa? This article compares the Spectrum against its demoscene peers to highlight each platform's unique strengths and constraints.

---

## Planned Content

- ZX Spectrum vs Commodore 64: attribute clash vs hardware sprites, beeper/AY vs SID
- ZX Spectrum vs Amiga: 3.5 MHz Z80 vs 7 MHz 68000, no hardware vs blitter/copper/Audio
- ZX Spectrum vs Atari ST: shared AY chip, different architectures, ST as "Spectrum with steroids"
- ZX Spectrum vs MSX: similar Z80 + PSG, different video architectures
- What the Spectrum excels at: CPU-per-pixel effects, multicolor, 1-bit music
- What the Spectrum struggles with: hardware scrolling, sprites, sample playback
- Unique Spectrum techniques that don't exist on other platforms: multicolor/race-the-beam
- Shared heritage: AY/YM music formats cross-pollinating Spectrum and ST scenes
- Relative scene sizes and activity levels
