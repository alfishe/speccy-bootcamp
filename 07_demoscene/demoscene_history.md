# ZX Spectrum Demoscene History

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

The ZX Spectrum demoscene is one of the longest-running and most creative demo communities, spanning from the mid-1980s through the present day. This article traces the full history: Western origins, the Soviet explosion, and the modern revival.

- Soviet explosion (1993—2005): the Pentagon era, E-Mage, Extreme, Progress, Skrju
- Cross-pollination: Western and Soviet scenes influencing each other
- Platform decline and migration (2000—2010): death of real hardware, emulators
- Modern revival (2010—present): ZX Spectrum Next, FPGA, online competitions
- Major demo parties: Forever, CAFe, DiHAlt, Chaos Constructions, Syntax
