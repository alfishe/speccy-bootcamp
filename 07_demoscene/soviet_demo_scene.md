# Soviet Demo Scene

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

The Soviet/post-Soviet demo scene centered on the Pentagon clone developed its own distinctive aesthetic, techniques, and community — arguably the most active Spectrum demoscene in the world from the mid-1990s onward.

---

## Planned Content

- Historical context: Soviet computer industry, bootleg Spectrum clones, Pentagon dominance
- Key groups: E-Mage, Extreme, Progress, Skrju, Flash Inc., Digital Reality, BraveStorm
- Pentagon-centric development: targeting 128K + Pentagon + Beta Disk (TR-DOS)
- Distinctive aesthetic: Soviet demoscene visual style, different from C64/Amiga
- Notable works: "Ecstasy", "Illumination", "Twice", "Black Crow"
- Major parties: CAFe (Kiev), DiHAlt (Ryazan), Chaos Constructions (St. Petersburg)
- Language and community: Russian-language BBS networks, FidoNet echos
- Software culture: TR-DOS disk magazines, text-mode browsers, BBS doors
- Technical innovations originating from the Soviet scene
- Modern state: still active, transitions to emulators and FPGA
