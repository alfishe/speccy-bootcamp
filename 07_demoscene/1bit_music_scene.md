# 1-Bit Music Scene

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

The 1-bit beeper music scene is a dedicated subculture that creates complex multi-channel music from a single bit of output. From the 48K's internal speaker to modern beeper engines, this article covers the evolution, key figures, and techniques.

---

## Planned Content

- Origins: 1982 BEEP command, crude melodies, ROM driver limitations
- First breakthroughs: Henry's multi-channel experiments, early 2-channel engines
- Engine evolution: FuzzClick → Octode → Huby → ZX Poliphony → modern engines
- Key composers: irrlicht project, utz, Mr. BEEP, Rotter, Tim Follin
- The "1-Bit Forum" community and its role in engine development
- Technical challenges: cycle-exact timing, contention, maximum channel count
- Modern beeper engines: Pusher, Sq1, Squeeker, Phaser1 — 5+ channels from 1 bit
- Beeper music in games: legendary soundtracks (Agent X, Chronos, Crosswize)
- Beeper on non-Spectrum platforms: same 1-bit techniques on PC speaker, SAM Coupé
- Current state: active development, new engines, beeper music compos
