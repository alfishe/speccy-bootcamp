# Tracker History — Sound Tracker to Arkos Tracker 3

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

A comprehensive chronological history of ZX Spectrum AY music software, from the first on-Spectrum trackers to modern PC-based editors.

---

## Planned Content

- Sound Tracker (1990, Sergey Bulba): the original AY tracker, .STC format
- Sound Tracker Pro / Pro Tracker 1.x: improved editing, .STP format
- Pro Tracker 3.x (1996+, Sergey Bulba): the PT3 module format, the gold standard
- Vortex Tracker II (2002+, Sergey Bulba): Windows-based PT3 editor, still maintained
- SQ Tracker: Soviet alternative, different approach
- E-Tracker: another Soviet tracker
- Arkos Tracker 1 (2003, Julien Nevo): cross-platform, AKG/AKM/AKY player formats
- Arkos Tracker 2/3 (2017—present): modern UI, multi-PSG, unlimited channels
- Format lineage: how module formats evolved
- Impact on the music scene: which tracker dominated when
