# MoonSound (OPL4 / YMF278B)

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

MoonSound is a high-end sound expansion based on the Yamaha OPL4 (YMF278B), providing 24-channel wavetable synthesis and 18-channel FM synthesis (OPL3 compatible).

---

## Planned Content

- YMF278B architecture: 24 wavetable channels + 18 FM channels (OPL3)
- Wavetable synthesis: sample-based, 4-bit ADPCM, instrument bank
- FM synthesis: OPL3 2-operator/4-operator modes
- Port decoding and register interface
- Programming model: instrument selection, frequency, volume, panning
- Music software support
- Comparison: MoonSound vs AY vs General Sound
