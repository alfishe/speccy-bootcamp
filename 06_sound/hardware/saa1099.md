# SAA1099 — Philips PSG

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

The Philips SAA1099 is a 6-channel stereo sound chip used in the ZXM Soundcard and some SAM Coupé expansions. It offers more channels than the AY but was less widely adopted in the Spectrum scene.
