# Stereo Audio Modifications

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

The original ZX Spectrum outputs mono audio. The demoscene and music communities developed various stereo modifications — ABC, ACB, and BytesDelight routing — to separate the three AY channels left/right/center.

---

## Planned Content

- Why stereo: AY has 3 channels, mono mixes them — stereo separates for wider field
- ABC routing: Channel A=left, B=center, C=right
- ACB routing: Channel A=left, C=center, B=right (different musical feel)
- Hardware modifications: wiring the AY analog output through stereo amp
- Per-clone stereo implementations (Pentagon, Scorpion, ATM)
- Software implications: trackers assume a specific routing
- BytesDelight and other advanced modifications
