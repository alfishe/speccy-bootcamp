# Covox and SounDrive — DAC-Based Sample Playback

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

Covox is a simple 8-bit resistor-ladder DAC on a parallel port; SounDrive is a 4×8-bit DAC expansion. Both provide direct sample playback without the AY chip's logarithmic DAC limitations.
