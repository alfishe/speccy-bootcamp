# AY-3-8912 / YM2149F PSG

> **Status**: Stub — article not yet written

The AY-3-8910/8912/8913 and YM2149F are the most important sound chips in the ZX Spectrum ecosystem. The 128K and all major clones include one. This article covers the hardware: pinouts, register interface, clock domains, DAC characteristics, and per-model differences.

---

## Planned Content

- Chip variants: AY-3-8910 (40-pin), AY-3-8912 (28-pin), AY-3-8913 (24-pin), YM2149F (pin-compatible)
- Internal block diagram: tone generators, noise generator, envelope generator, mixer, DAC
- Register interface: BC1/BDIR control pins, address/data latch protocol
- Clock domains: 1.7734 MHz (ZX 128K), 1.75 MHz (Pentagon), 2.0 MHz (Atari ST)
- DAC characteristics: logarithmic 16-level, resistor-ladder implementation
- Per-model port decoding (#FFFD/#BFFFD on ZX, mirrors on clones)
- Electrical: analog output circuit, mixing, DC blocking
